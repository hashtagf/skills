# Reference library — pages that look designed

Rendered from the Apache-2.0 templates in the Open Design repository (github.com/nexu-io/open-design, fetched 2026-11-05; copyright its authors, see NOTICE). These are the **visual examples of good work** in this skill: look at the ones for your page type before building, then at your direction's preview. They are references for composition, type voice and restraint, not templates to copy: never reuse their names, copy or imagery in client work.

Rows marked *Our own build* were made with this skill and checked by its scripts; the rest come from Open Design. Each has a first-screen picture (`<name>.jpg`, 1440×900) and a longer one (`<name>-full.jpg`). `comp` names the composition in `scripts/compositions.js` that distils it.

## Reading them

- **One decisive move per page**: a giant numeral, a full-bleed photograph, a three-line serif headline. Everything else is quiet.
- **Type voice before colour**: roman with an italic phrase, tiny mono register labels, serif numerals for numbers that matter.
- **Real imagery or none**: a photograph carries the page, or type and rules do all the work. No clip-art illustration.
- **Product pages are plain in chrome, not in character**: dashboards, pricing and docs get beauty from spacing, a real font pair, a brand mark and one signature element (a first-run hero, a diagram, a status story); not from effects.
- **Content belongs to a subject**: every reference speaks about one real thing with specific nouns and numbers.
