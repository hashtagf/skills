Section orders observed:
- SaaS: hero → logos → bento → 3 steps → stats → testimonials → pricing → FAQ → CTA.
- Local business: hero + offer → why → services/classes → schedule → pricing → people → reviews → location → FAQ → offer again.
- Brand/agency: wordmark or photo hero → selected work 01–03 → manifesto → stats → contact.

Most refined templates (@larsen66: Pilates, Dental, Yuko, Glass, CRM; ReactHive:
Nivara, Solenne, Chispa, Morrow) mostly avoid library effects and win on
photography, type and domain-specific components.
