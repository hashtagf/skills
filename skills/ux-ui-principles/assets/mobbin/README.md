# Mobbin web apps — catalog (Latest, ranks 50–100)

Our own notes on ranks 50–100 of <https://mobbin.com/discover/apps/web/latest> as listed on 2026-11-07 (51 apps,
four screens each, read through a logged-in BrowserOS session). Mobbin's screens are **real products' UI**, so this
set complements the [showcase](../showcase/README.md) (generated pages) and the [references](../references/README.md):
use it for **app screens** — dashboards, editors, inboxes, chat, sign-in, onboarding — and for the landing page each
product puts in front of them.

**How to use:** `python3 scripts/look.py <tag>` (e.g. `crm`, `finance`, `ai-chat`, `auth`, `canvas`) lists the
pictures of the `template` and `study` rows with that tag; `look.py product` / `landing` / `auth` include them after
the references and the showcase. Open the pictures, name the one move you take, then build it in the page's own
direction. Map the move onto the routed rules first ([app-screen rules](../../references/app-screen-rules.md),
[back office](../../references/back-office.md), [AI features](../../references/ai-features.md),
[layout index](../layouts/index.md)) — a pattern seen here never overrides a rule there.

## Rules

- **Take the move, not the look.** Never reuse an app's name, logo, copy, illustration, photography or brand
  colour in client work. "Kanban card with a favicon per company" is a move; their purple is not.
- The pictures and `urls.json` are a **local study set** (git-ignored): the screens are other companies' products
  captured by Mobbin under its own terms. Do not commit, publish or ship them.
- A product's choice is evidence of what that team shipped, not proof it works: check the move against the rule
  files before adopting it, and say which picture it came from.

## Rebuild

1. Open `https://mobbin.com/discover/apps/web/latest` in a logged-in BrowserOS tab. The list is virtualised: scroll
   from the top in steps and collect each card's `h3` name, app link and its `img[src*="file.webp"]` screens into a
   map (cards that play a video carry no images: open the app's `/screens` page and take its first four).
2. Write `{"<slug>": [url1, url2, url3, url4], ...}` to `assets/mobbin/urls.json` (slug = lowercase name, runs of
   non-alphanumerics → `-`), in rank order.
3. `python3 scripts/mobbin_fetch.py` downloads and resizes them (exit 1 if any failed; signed URLs expire — collect
   again). New ranks: add rows to `catalog.json` only after opening every picture; write the note in our words.
